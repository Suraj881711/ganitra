{{ t.t_arithmetic }}

Fractions, percentages, BODMAS — with reduction steps.

Method: Work step by step and show the working — for HCF and LCM, break both numbers into prime factors first.

Example: Find the HCF of 12 and 18 → 6

✖️

{{ t.t_algebra }}

Equations, systems, inequalities, factor, expand, simplify.

Method: Keep the equation balanced — do the same to both sides until the unknown stands alone.

Example: Solve x + 7 = 15 → x = 8

📈

{{ t.t_exponents_logs }}

Powers, logarithms and log equations.

Method: Same base? Add or subtract the powers. For logs, turn the log question into a power question.

Example: Simplify 2^3 * 2^2 → 32

📐

{{ t.t_trigonometry }}

Values, identities, equations and inverse trig (degrees by default).

Method: Learn the standard-angle table, then fix the sign by the quadrant (ASTC).

Example: Find the value of sin 150 degrees → 1/2

∫

{{ t.t_calculus }}

Limits, derivatives (partial & implicit), integrals, ODEs.

Method: Power rule for derivatives, reverse power rule for integrals — adjust the power by one each time.

Example: Integrate x^3 → x^4/4 + C

🔀

{{ t.t_transforms }}

Laplace & inverse Laplace, Taylor/Maclaurin series, partial fractions.

Method: A series writes a function as a sum of powers of x; Laplace turns a function of t into a function of s.

Example: Find the Taylor series of e^x → 1 + x + x^2/2 + x^3/6 + …

🔢

{{ t.t_linear_algebra }}

Determinant, inverse, eigenvalues, rank, Ax = b.

Method: The determinant of [[a, b], [c, d]] is ad − bc; systems are solved by elimination or the matrix inverse.

Example: Find the determinant of the matrix [[3, 4], [1, 2]] → 2

🔷

{{ t.t_geometry }}

Areas, volumes and coordinate geometry.

Method: Pick the formula for the shape — area, volume, circumference — put the measurements in, and keep the units right.

Example: Find the area of a circle with radius 7 cm → 49π ≈ 153.94

🎲

{{ t.t_statistics }}

Mean, median, mode, SD, dice, coins, cards, counting.

Method: Sort the data first: the median is the middle value, the mean is total ÷ count, the mode is the most frequent.

Example: Find the median of 5, 3, 8, 7, 2 → 5

📊

{{ t.t_distributions }}

Normal (z-scores, percentiles), Poisson and binomial probabilities.

Method: For repeated trials use nCr × p^r × (1 − p)^(n − r); for the normal curve use z = (x − mean) / SD.

Example: A coin is tossed 5 times — probability of exactly 3 heads → 5/16

#️⃣

{{ t.t_number_theory }}

Primes, GCD/LCM with factorisation steps, modulo.

Method: Prime factorisation unlocks almost everything here: HCF, LCM, factor counts and divisibility.

Example: How many factors does 36 have? → 9

🔁

{{ t.t_sequences }}

AP/GP terms and sums, natural-number series.

Method: AP adds the same difference (nth term = a + (n − 1)d); GP multiplies by the same ratio (nth term = a × r^(n − 1)).

Example: Find the 10th term of the AP 2, 5, 8, 11 → 29

ℹ️

{{ t.t_complex }}

Arithmetic, modulus, conjugate, argument.

Method: Treat i as a number with i² = −1: combine the real parts and the imaginary parts separately.

Example: Find the modulus of 3 + 4i → 5

🧮

{{ t.t_sets }}

Union, intersection, difference, symmetric difference.

Method: Union takes everything from both sets with no repeats; intersection takes only what is common.

Example: A = {1, 2, 3}, B = {3, 4, 5} — A union B → {1, 2, 3, 4, 5}

🔄

{{ t.t_units }}

Length, mass, time, volume, area and temperature.

Method: Multiply when going to a smaller unit, divide for a bigger one: km → m is ×1000, minutes → hours is ÷60.

Example: Convert 5 km into metres → 5000 metres

📝

{{ t.t_word_problems }}

Percent change, speed–distance, simple interest.

Method: Name the unknown, turn the story into an equation or a direct formula, then solve it.

Example: Simple interest on Rs 1000 at 5% for 2 years → Rs 100

📉

{{ t.t_graphs }}

Plot one or two functions; shaded area for definite integrals.

Method: A line is y = mx + c: m is the slope (steepness) and c is where the line crosses the y-axis.

Example: Equation of the line through the origin with slope 2 → y = 2x
